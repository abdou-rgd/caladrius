# Public oracle

Versioned ground truth for the conformance tests (see `AGENTS.md` section 5).

- `data/`: public datasets as CSV with their origin and license in `data/README.md`.
- `scripts/`: R scripts that compute the expected results (PKNCA), with the options written explicitly and the package versions recorded.
- `expected/`: the generated expected results. Regenerate with the script; never edit by hand.

Vendor project files, exported tables and screenshots do not belong in this repository. `.gitignore` and `cargo xtask lint` (`vendor-export`) keep them out.

## Layout

- `data/*.csv`, `expected/*.csv` and `expected/*.options.json` at the top level: the NCA cases (PKNCA), read by `cargo xtask conformance`.
- `expected/models/`: exact closed-form values of the six one-compartment models, from `scripts/models_closed_form.R` (256-bit arithmetic with Rmpfr, cross-checked against a matrix exponential and an ODE solver). One case per model and parameter set: `model_<case>.csv` (`subject,parameter,value`, the subject being `t=<time>` or `scalar`) and `.options.json`.
- `expected/models/pk2/`: the two-compartment models (task T-032, `specs/models.md` section 11), from `scripts/models_2c_closed_form.R` and `scripts/models_2c_derivatives.R` (sourced by the first). Same long format and options files as the one-compartment cases, in a subdirectory, read by their own loaders (`caladrius_testkit::pk2`) and by `cargo xtask conformance`, which reports them in `docs/conformance.md` since the engine and interface cards for two compartments (T-033, T-034). Three kinds of cases: `model_pk2_<id>_<case>` (`conc`, `auc`, `aumc` on a time grid and the scalars: the three parameter sets, half-lives, volumes, areas to infinity, MRT, Tmax and Cmax), `model_pk2_deriv_<id>_<case>` (partial derivatives of the concentration with respect to the parameters of the set the case is given in and to `ka`, `tlag`, `dur`, from 256-bit central differences; entries with a condition number above 1e3 are omitted, see the options) and `model_pk2_errors` (what the engine must refuse, as not-available rows with the reason and the words of the message). The concentrations are independent explicit sums of exponentials in 256-bit arithmetic (Rmpfr), cross-checked on every case against a matrix exponential (expm) and an ODE solver (deSolve lsoda). The script also writes `crates/caladrius-models/tests/oracle_models_2c.cases`, the list of cases the engine test runs.
- `expected/models/md/`: multiple dosing and steady state (task T-047, `specs/models.md` section 12, open item OM-16), for the twelve ids `pk1.*` and `pk2.*`, from `scripts/models_md.R` (about 10 minutes). Same long format and options files; loaders: `caladrius_testkit::md`. Four kinds of cases: `model_md_<family>_<id>_sched_n<k>` (a schedule of k = 1, 3, 10, 40 doses with irregular times, varying amounts, unsorted records, a zero dose and, for the inputs with a duration, overlapping infusions of different durations: `conc` and `auc` by time, scalar `auc_inf`), `model_md_<family>_<id>_regular_n<k>` (k equal doses at a regular interval, MOD-MD-07), `model_md_<family>_<id>_ss_<case>` (the steady state of a regular regimen over one interval: `conc`, `auc` and `accum_c` by time since the dose and the scalars `cmax_ss`, `tmax_ss`, `cmin_ss`, `cav_ss`, `auc_tau_ss`, `accum_cmax`, `accum_auc`; intervals from k·tau = 1e-3 to 50, ka/k = 500, 1, 1 + 1e-6, 1 + 1e-3, flip-flop, lags below, equal to and above tau, tau = T) and `model_md_<family>_<id>_conv_<case>` (the two convention points of OM-17: the steady-state lag forms of S-36 that need tau >= tlag (+ T), with the printed branch evaluated in the options; and the non-strict inequalities tau = T, touching and overlapping infusions), plus `model_md_errors` (what the engine must refuse, MOD-MD-12, as not-available rows with the reason). The expected values are plain sums of the single-dose closed forms in 256-bit arithmetic (Rmpfr), rounded once to double; at steady state the sum over doses is taken term by term to a bounded tail (below 2^-70 of the smallest value), and the geometric-series closed forms of the specification are implemented a second time in the script and checked against the sums (agreement 2^-60) instead of being used to produce a value. Every case is cross-checked within 1e-8 by an ODE solution (deSolve, lsoda) with the doses as events and, at steady state, by the periodic solution of the linear system and by a long train of dosing events. The regimen is stored in the options (`regimen`); the script also writes `crates/caladrius-models/tests/oracle_models_md.cases`, the list of cases the engine test runs.
- `expected/fit/` and `data/fit/`: reference weighted least-squares fits, from `scripts/fit_wls.R` (`stats::nls` and `minpack.lm::nlsLM`, statistics from the formulas of `specs/fit.md`). One case per dataset and weighting: `fit_<dataset>_<weighting>.csv` and `.options.json`. Datasets: `theoph`, `indometh`, `spec` (the five-point example), and, since task T-029, the synthetic `infusion`, `zero_order` and `lag` profiles of `data/fit/synthetic_*.csv` (written by the same script, fixed seed, for `pk1.iv_infusion`, `pk1.oral_0` and `pk1.oral_1_lag`; the options file has the fixed parameters in `fixed` and the generation in `generated_data`).
- Regenerate: `Rscript oracle/scripts/nca_pknca.R`, `Rscript oracle/scripts/models_closed_form.R`, `Rscript oracle/scripts/models_2c_closed_form.R` (about 30 minutes: 256-bit central differences), `Rscript oracle/scripts/models_md.R`, `Rscript oracle/scripts/fit_wls.R`, `Rscript oracle/scripts/fit_ml.R`, `Rscript oracle/scripts/data_population.R`; all seven are deterministic and rewrite the files byte for byte (except `legacy_notes.json`, which is a pinned snapshot).
- `expected/fit/ml/` and `data/fit/ml/`: reference maximum-likelihood fits with an estimated error model (task T-148, `specs/fit.md` section 13, rules FIT-ML-*), from `scripts/fit_ml.R` (about 7 minutes). One case per dataset and error model: `ml_<dataset>_<model>.csv` (`subject,parameter,value`: estimates, `m2ll`, `aic_ml`, `bic_ml`, the expected and observed information standard errors and correlations, `sigma`, `iwres` by observation and their summaries, `status`), `.options.json` (the call, the starts, the seed, the software versions, the accuracy check) and `.gnls.csv` (the `nlme::gnls` fit, informational only, never a test). Error models: `additive`, `proportional`, `combined`, `combined_sd`. Datasets: the F data of the specification, one seeded profile per kind (`iv_s1` to `iv_s4`, `oral_s1` to `oral_s4`, in `data/fit/ml/`), Theoph and Indometh, and the at-bound and collapse cases (`ml_f_kbound_*`, `ml_f_combined_start_at_collapse`) and the cases with the sample at time 0 (`ml_theoph_t0_combined`, `ml_theoph_t0_combined_sd`, compared with `ml_theoph_additive` on the same rows: the subjects whose sample at time 0 is exactly 0 are not in them, they are refused, see `errors.json`). `errors.json` lists what the engine must refuse (`exact_fit`, `non_positive_sigma`, `too_few_observations`, `unbounded_likelihood` for an observation of 0 where the prediction is 0 under a combined error model, an unknown error model or `error_init`) with the words of the message, and, where a valid variant exists (a valid `error_init`, the additive model), the `control_options` the engine must accept on the same observations. The estimate is found with an analytic gradient by `stats::optim` from 27 seeded starts and then polished by Newton steps at 256 bits (Rmpfr) with a central-difference Hessian; the gradient at the written 15-digit point is checked at 256 bits and the sizes are in each options file. The script also writes `crates/caladrius-fit/tests/oracle_fit_ml.cases`, the list of cases the engine test runs, and the testkit reads the files with `caladrius_testkit::ml`. `ml_theoph_gt0_additive` is the additive fit of Theoph on the rows with time > 0, the rows of the combined Theoph cases, so that a collapsed combined fit is compared with a one-parameter fit on the same rows; `scripts/ml_collapse_check.R` (run at the end of `fit_ml.R`, or by itself) checks that property in R and stops on a mismatch. The lagged routes (`ml6f_*` rows of `errors.json`): on `pk1.oral_1_lag` and `pk1.oral_0_lag` the prediction is 0 for every t <= tlag and `tlag` has no upper bound by default, so a leading zero at a time t0 > 0 (every observation up to t0 is 0) makes the likelihood unbounded under `combined`, `combined_sd` and `proportional` when the domain of `tlag` reaches t0; `scripts/ml_lag_unbounded.R` (sourced by `fit_ml.R`) proves it at 256 bits on a one-subject dataset (`data/fit/ml/lag_oral_1.csv`, `lag_oral_0.csv`: the `oral_s1` recipe, seed 1, generated by the lag model with tlag = 0.5, first sample at t = 0.5 set to exactly 0) and writes the points of the paths to `expected/fit/ml/ml6f_lag_paths.json`; each row has `controls` (an upper bound on `tlag` below t0, a zero that follows a nonzero sample, a fixed `tlag` below t0) that the engine must not refuse with the code `unbounded_likelihood`.
- `data/population/` and `expected/data/`: the dataset oracle for `specs/data.md` (task T-148, OD-01 to OD-05), from `scripts/data_population.R` (a few seconds; `nlme` only). `data/population/cases.json` lists the cases; `data/population/*.csv` are the files (event-layout files that must load, one file per refusal DAT-ERR-01 to 06, profile-layout files, and Theoph and Indometh in both layouts). `expected/data/<case>.json` holds, per case, the delimiter, the roles, the notes that must appear, the subjects, the doses and observations with their rows, the counts and the covariates (kind `data_load`), or the code, the row, the subject and the words of the sentence, and the records of the subjects the problem does not affect (kind `data_refusal`). The data facts are computed in R from the file and cross-checked with `nlme::groupedData` and `nlme::getGroups`; a disagreement stops the script. `expected/data/legacy_notes.json` is not generated: it pins the notes, checks and number of readings that the importer gave before the brick for every profile-layout file, written once from a throwaway test (commit named inside; the steps and the code of the test are in `expected/data/README.md`). The script also writes `crates/caladrius-project/tests/oracle_data.cases`.
- `expected/pop/` and `data/pop/`: the population-fit oracle (task T-152, `specs/population.md`, rules POP-ORA-*), written before any engine code exists. The 256-bit machinery (Rmpfr: the conditional mode by Newton with exact jets, the five objective forms, CWRES in three forms, the exact marginal for one random effect by the trapezoidal rule with an ESTIMATED error bound: the Richardson-style quantity |T(h)/T(h/2) - 1| plus a truncation term whose supremum of the likelihood is the largest grid value times e, a heuristic margin, so it is an estimate and not a proof) is `scripts/pop_lib.R`; the test-only model with the identity link (POP-MOD-05) is `identity_linear`. Inputs are decimal strings, expected values 50-digit strings rounded from 256 bits (exact cases) or the nearest double (the others). Loaders: `caladrius_testkit::pop`; an independent double-precision implementation of the same objective, `caladrius_testkit::naive_pop`, reproduces the files (`crates/caladrius-testkit/tests/pop_consistency.rs`).
  - `expected/pop/exact/` (`scripts/pop_exact.R`, about 30 minutes, 256 bits): the exact identification suite, 29 cases in four families: `lin_*` (linear in the random effects, where the linearised form is the exact marginal), `nl_*` (nonlinear, additive-type identities), `frz_*` (the variance held at the population prediction or at the fixed point of the mode, OP-14) and `int_*` (the interaction, where the three forms split). `lin_px1`, `nl_px5` and `int_px2` are the worked examples PX1, PX5 and PX2 of the specification.
  - `expected/pop/ofv/` (`scripts/pop_ofv.R`, about 2 hours 10 minutes: the Theoph cases take 17 to 20 minutes each): 20 cases, the objective at fixed parameters in double precision (from 256-bit modes, with the checks of the file) with the diagnostics of POP-DIA-*, for `pk1.iv_bolus`, `pk1.oral_1` and `pk2.iv_bolus` by the four error models on seeded synthetic data (`syn_*`), and Theoph (`theoph_*`) and Indometh (`indometh_*`) with the model that fits them.
  - `expected/pop/errors.json` and `data/pop/errors/` (`scripts/pop_errors.R`): the refusals of POP-INN-03 and PX4 as rows, each refused row with a control that must not be refused; `words_basis` says whether the words come from the spec, from a reused individual rule or are inferred.
  - `expected/pop/sealed/` and `data/pop/sealed/` (`scripts/pop_sealed.R`): the sealed simulate-then-fit data (POP-ORA-04), two designs of 200 replicates, one CSV per replicate, the SHA-256 of every file and of the manifest in `<design>.json`. Committed before any engine code; never edited, regenerated or re-rolled; the tests recompute the hashes and compare the two manifest hashes with values pinned in `crates/caladrius-fit/tests/oracle_pop.rs`. `pop_sealed.R` stops with an error, before it deletes anything, when its outputs exist.
  - `expected/pop/second_look/` (`scripts/pop_nlme.R` (about 1 second), `scripts/pop_saemix.R` (about 35 seconds, seed 632545, saemix 3.5), informational: 10 cases each, additive and proportional error only, never a pass/fail reference) and `scripts/pop_nlmixr2.R` (written, not run: nlmixr2 is not installed, Q-021; every statement about it in the script is `documented, untested`).
  - `scripts/pop_index.R` writes `crates/caladrius-fit/tests/oracle_pop.cases`, the list of cases `crates/caladrius-fit/tests/oracle_pop.rs` runs. Regenerate: `Rscript oracle/scripts/pop_exact.R`, `pop_ofv.R`, `pop_errors.R`, `pop_nlme.R`, `pop_saemix.R`, `pop_index.R` (all deterministic; `pop_sealed.R` is run once and its output is sealed).
  - Pending until `pop_exact.R` and `pop_ofv.R` are next regenerated (they are not rerun for this alone, and `pop_lib.R` is not edited before then because the hashes of both suites depend on it): the field `ofv_error_bound` / `rel_error_bound` of the exact-marginal block should be labelled `estimated_*` in the JSON, and the dead assignment `E <- pc$yhat - mpfr(vapply(...))` in `lin_form` (`pop_lib.R`, overwritten two lines later) should be removed.
