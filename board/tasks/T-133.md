## Goal

Today one plot has two drawings: egui_plot on the screen and `crates/caladrius-engine/src/svg.rs` for `export.plot`. They differ in ranges, ticks, tick labels, log axis, hidden points, colours, roles and legend (`docs/interface-inventory.md`, Plots). This card makes the scene a piece of data that the engine builds once and that any front draws: the SVG export, the egui painter, or SVG in a web page. The three prototypes of wave 3 draw this scene and no chart library.

1. **The scene.** A new module `crates/caladrius-engine/src/scene.rs`: a serializable `PlotScene`, built from the result of an analysis.
   - Panels with their title, their axes (title, linear or log) and their range. A log axis is already in log10, so a front only applies an affine map from scene units to pixels.
   - Ticks with their value and their label, by the rules `svg.rs` follows today (nice steps, whole decades, the labels it writes now).
   - Series with their role, style and points.
   - The points a log axis cannot show, and the sentence that says so.
   - The guides (identity, zero) and the legend entries.
   - No pixel and no colour: a role names what a series is, and the theme of whoever draws decides how it looks.
2. **The SVG export on top of the scene.** `svg.rs` draws from `PlotScene` alone. `export.plot` stays byte-identical for every plot it exports today:
   - capture the SVG of a set of cases into golden files before the refactor: linear, log, both, a fit with its predicted values, residuals with the zero guide, observed against predicted with the identity guide, points below the limit, a log panel with nothing left to show, and a custom panel size;
   - the golden test compares bytes;
   - the digest workload calls `export.plot` too (`xtask/src/digest/workload.rs`, session `plots`).
3. **The NCA scene.** The NCA screen's plot becomes a scene too. It is built from an NCA analysis and one subject and holds:
   - the observed profile, with the NCA roles of each point: used for λz, excluded, below the limit, replaced, and the start of the λz range;
   - the λz line from the intercept and λz of the answer, from the first point of the phase to the last time. This is a drawing of two engine values, evaluated with `libm` (rule 10), and no new estimate. The screen does the same today in `crates/caladrius-ui/src/nca.rs` (around line 950) with the standard `exp`.
4. **The command `plot.scene`** `{analysis, subject?, log?}` returns the scene of a plot analysis or of an NCA analysis (`subject` names one subject of an NCA analysis). It is in the registry with its parameter and result schemas, and it carries a seal like every other command. `export.plot` also accepts an NCA analysis with a `subject`, drawn from the same scene, so that the screen of each prototype can be compared with its export.
