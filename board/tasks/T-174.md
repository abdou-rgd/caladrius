## Goal

The engine's FOCEI fit of the 200 sealed replicates of `accurate` gives a mean cl of 5.0796 against the truth 5:
- the bias is +0.0796, about 4.4 standard errors of the mean, against a limit of 0.0538;
- ka and v pass;
- every fit ends with a Newton decrement below 1e-8;
- the OFV at fixed parameters matches the 256-bit oracle of T-153.

These numbers come from the T-154 note.

This card says whether the bias is a property of the FOCEI estimator on this design or a defect of the engine. It uses open tools only (`AGENTS.md` rule 1), and nothing is installed:
- `saemix` and `nlme` are installed;
- `nlmixr2` is not (Q-021).

1. **An independent FOCEI fit in R, in double precision**, written by this card from `specs/population.md`:
   - the objective: POP-OBJ-02, FOCEI with interaction, the `full` convention, combined error σ² = a² + b²·ŷ²;
   - the outer vector: POP-EST-01;
   - the prediction functions of `oracle/scripts/pop_lib.R` may be reused.

   Run it on each of the 200 replicates of `oracle/expected/pop/sealed/accurate.json`, with the request of the sealed test (start at the truth, unless the sealed file says otherwise).

   Show that it reaches the minimum:
   - a final gradient or decrement;
   - two starts on at least 10 replicates;
   - the bound A = a² ≥ 0 handled as a bound.

   Before the sealed data, check its OFV at fixed parameters against at least two cases of `oracle/expected/pop/ofv/`, to 1e-8 relative.
2. **saemix on the same replicates**, as a second look. POP-ORA-03 calls it a different estimator, with the exact likelihood by stochastic approximation. Use the settings of `oracle/scripts/pop_saemix.R`, with a fixed seed.
3. **Replicate by replicate against the engine.**
   - The engine's numbers are in a CSV written by the T-154 agent; the orchestrator gives the path. They are data.
   - Never read `crates/caladrius-fit/src/`.
   - For each replicate, give the difference of each θ between the R FOCEI fit and the engine.
   - Give also the R OFV evaluated at the engine's estimate, minus the R OFV at the R estimate. A positive gap above the R fit's own tolerance means the engine stopped short of the optimum.
4. **The aggregates** of cl, v and ka under each of the three fits (engine, R FOCEI, saemix): the mean, the bias, the limit 3·sd/√K and the median.
5. **A verdict with its numbers**, one of:
   - (a) FOCEI itself is biased on this design: R FOCEI agrees with the engine replicate by replicate, and the bias belongs to the estimator. Say what saemix gives.
   - (b) An engine defect: the R fit finds lower OFVs or different θ.
   - (c) Undecided: say what is missing.

Never change a sealed file, a constant of `check_sealed_recovery` or a tolerance.
