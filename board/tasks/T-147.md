1. **Maximum likelihood with an estimated residual error model** (`specs/fit.md`, a new section, ids `FIT-ML-*`). Today a fit is weighted least squares with fixed weights (sections 2 to 8). Add:
   - the error models: additive (σ_i = a), proportional (σ_i = b·ŷ_i), and combined. Name both combined forms that open tools use (the variance sum a² + b²·ŷ² and the standard-deviation sum a + b·ŷ), choose one as the default with the reason, and say whether the other is offered;
   - the objective −2 log L(θ, ξ) = Σ [ln(2π·σ_i²) + (y_i − ŷ_i)²/σ_i²], with the constant kept, and its relation to extended least squares;
   - how it is minimised, written as rules the engine can follow with what `caladrius-fit` already has (Gauss-Newton or Levenberg-Marquardt steps, FIT-CNV-*). The objective is not a plain sum of squares, so say exactly what is iterated, how the error parameters are scaled (a log scale or bounds above 0), and why it converges where it does;
   - the exact cases that give hand checks. With the additive model, θ̂ is the unweighted least-squares estimate and â² = RSS/N. With the proportional model, the result differs from `inv_yhat2` reweighting; say how (OF-04);
   - initial estimates for the error parameters;
   - convergence, and what is refused, each with a sentence:
     - σ_i ≤ 0 at an observation (the proportional model with ŷ ≤ 0);
     - an exact fit, where the likelihood is unbounded;
     - N ≤ P + Q;
     - an error parameter going to its bound;
   - the outputs:
     - −2LL;
     - AIC = −2LL + 2·(P + Q) and BIC = −2LL + (P + Q)·ln N, next to the WRSS forms of FIT-OUT-07, never replacing them;
     - the covariance matrix of all estimates (observed or expected information: choose, say why, and say which one brick 3 will reuse);
     - standard errors and correlations;
   - **IWRES** (item c): (y_i − ŷ_i)/σ_i at the estimate, in the residual table, with the summaries an analyst reads (mean, standard deviation, the count beyond ±2 and ±3). Say what is NC.
   - BLQ: observations stay discarded as in FIT-DAT-01. Name the censored-likelihood method (M3) as an open item for brick 3, not a rule.
   - Hand-checked worked examples, as in section 11.
