## Goal

Write `specs/vpc.md` from allowed sources. It specifies a visual predictive check on top of the simulator of `specs/simulation.md` and the population fit of `specs/population.md`. It answers, in order:

1. **What is simulated.**
   - Simulation from the estimates of a population fit (θ̂, Ω̂, ξ̂ of POP-EST-04) on the design of the observed dataset (the observed times, doses and subjects, as SIM-MOD-04 item 1 reads it), with R replicates and a seed (SIM-RNG-02).
   - Parameter uncertainty (OS-09, purpose p = 3 reserved) stays out of v1 unless you argue otherwise. Give the reason.
2. **Binning.**
   - The rules offered: by nominal time, by user-given edges, by equal counts. Choose a default and give the reason.
   - Which edge a value on a boundary belongs to.
   - What x-value a bin is drawn at.
   - What happens to an empty bin, or a bin with too few observations.
3. **The statistics per bin.**
   - The observed quantiles (type 7, as SIM-SUM-01).
   - The same quantiles on each replicate.
   - Then the band across replicates for each of those quantiles.
   - Name the default probabilities of the prediction interval and the level of the band, with the reason.
   - Say whether the observed quantile is computed over all rows in the bin or one value per subject.
4. **Prediction correction (pcVPC).** The normalisation of Bergstrand et al. (2011), the bin's median PRED over the row's PRED, with the variant for a lower bound if the paper gives one. State it as a named option that changes the data and is never applied silently.
5. **BLQ and censoring.**
   - Observed rows below the LLOQ against the BLQ rule of SIM-ORD-05 in the simulation.
   - The fraction below the LLOQ per bin, observed against simulated, with its band (cite the source).
   - How BLQ rows enter, or do not enter, the quantiles of item 3.
6. **Stratification.** By a covariate column, by route or by dose group. Is each stratum binned on its own? What is refused?
7. **The outputs.** The tables the interface draws (T-156 or later), with their columns. No drawing is specified here, only the numbers.
8. **Determinism.**
   - The VPC result is a pure function of (fit estimates, design, seed, R, options).
   - It is the same on 1 thread, N threads and wasm, by SIM-DET-01 to -04. Say what is added to the canonical text.
9. **What can be tested, and how.**
   - **Exact part:** given a simulated table, binning and quantiles are a deterministic function. That is a base-R oracle with no package.
   - **Calibration:** under the true model, the observed quantile falls inside its band at a rate with a stated binomial bound, at a fixed seed.
   - **pcVPC:** the normalisation, on a worked case.
   - **Refusals.**
10. **Refusals:** R too small for the band level, a bin rule that leaves no bin, a stratum with no observation, pcVPC with a non-positive PRED, and limits on sizes.
11. **Open items for Abdou,** each with a proposal.

Every rule gets an id (`VPC-*`) and a grade (`assumed`, `documented, untested`), as in the other specs. Write worked examples with numbers computed in R 4.5.2 (base R only, seeded), as hand checks. "Aucun chiffre inventé": every number comes from a computation you ran, and its script lives in the session scratchpad.

Read `specs/simulation.md` (in full, section 8 first), then `specs/population.md` (POP-EST, POP-DIA), `specs/data.md` (BLQ, LLOQ columns) and `AGENTS.md`.
