# T-132: Three visual directions of the NCA screen

- **Role:** interface (on Opus for this card: the visual direction is a design judgement, and the three prototypes will copy it)
- **State:** in progress
- **Iteration:** T-130
- **Depends on:** none
- **Source:** iteration 2 (T-130), wave 1.

## Goal

Three mockups of the same real screen, each a self-contained HTML page with a light and a dark theme. Abdou picks one, and its tokens become the single token file of the app and the site.

**The screen** is the NCA of one subject from `oracle/data`, from the data to the results (UX-REQ-01):

- the data table;
- the concentration-time plot in semi-log, with points below the limit of quantification marked, the λz points highlighted, and the range chosen by clicking or dragging on the curve (UX-LZ-01);
- the results table, with the flags and their visible thresholds (UX-FR-04, UX-FR-12), the seal under each result (a short hash), and the stale state shown after an input changes;
- two docked panels and a hint of the command palette (UX-FR-01).

The mockup shows real numbers: every value comes from a run of `caladrius-cli` on that subject. None is typed by hand, and the command used is written in the page source as a comment. Executables under `target/` cannot write files on this PC, so read the CLI output from stdout.

**The directions:**

- A, "Workbench": from Abdou's site mockup https://claude.ai/artifact/LQthMwzUM6Sj5WSeJxEcdh (night, brass, paper; Amiri, Alegreya Sans, IBM Plex Mono).
- B, "Paper": light, built from the logo green and the site tokens (`site/src/*.css` of the umbrella, checked out in `C:\Users\abdou\officina-umbrella`).
- C, "Instrument": dense and keyboard-first, for advanced users, in both themes.

Each direction comes with its tokens in the shape of `crates/caladrius-ui/src/theme.json`, so that the chosen one drops in. Fonts come from Google Fonts, under their open licences.

## Allowed files

Write only under `C:\Users\abdou\apothicaire\notes\iteration-2-mockups\`, outside every repository: `a-workbench.html`, `b-paper.html`, `c-instrument.html`, and one `tokens-<direction>.json` each. ## Completion criterion

- The three pages open offline, apart from the fonts.
- Each page shows the whole screen at 1440×900 and stays usable at 390 px wide.
- Every number in a page appears in the stdout of the CLI command written in that page.
- The orchestrator publishes the three pages as Artifacts and writes their links in this card.
- A report of at most ten lines: what sets each direction apart, its token count, and one bolder next step.
