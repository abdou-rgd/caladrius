The new rules of `AGENTS.md` sections 8 and 9 become mechanical checks, each with a stable id and tests on fixtures:

- `board-iteration` (in `xtask/src/lint/board.rs`): `- **Iteration:** T-xxx` names a card whose header says `- **Kind:** iteration`; `Kind` has no other value; an iteration card has a `## Brief` section with `**Goal.**`, `**Deliverables.**`, `**Claims touched.**`, `**Done when.**` and `**Plan.**`; a `done` iteration has a `## Outcome` section with `**Bolder next step.**` and a `**Learning page.**` that holds a link, and none of its cards is `todo`, `in progress` or `to review`.

`cargo xtask review` reads neither the contract nor the board: unchanged.
