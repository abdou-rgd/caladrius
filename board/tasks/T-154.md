## Goal

Implement the outer fit of `specs/population.md` on top of `caladrius_fit::pop`, so that the oracle rows that wait for it pass.

1. **The outer vector and its transforms (POP-EST-01, -02):**
   - ln θ for a typical value with a random effect, θ on its own scale otherwise;
   - Ω in log-Cholesky form (diagonal: ln ω_k; block: L with exp on the diagonal);
   - ξ on the variance scale with bound 0;
   - `fixed` for θ, an error parameter (keys `a`, `b`), a diagonal ω_k or a whole block; `bounds` as FIT-BND-01, -03.
2. **The two-stage start (POP-EST-05):**
   - one ML fit per subject with `subject = all`;
   - the subjects left out of the means are listed;
   - the errors of item 3, with their sentences;
   - `start` per parameter (user, two_stage, mixed).
3. **The outer optimiser (POP-EST-03):**
   - BFGS with the active set of FIT-BND-01, and backtracking as in FIT-ML-07;
   - a central-difference gradient, each evaluation re-solving the inner problems to the polish floor with a warm start per subject;
   - the stop by the Newton decrement of a confirmation Hessian, absolute;
   - the statuses (`inner_failed` included);
   - a trace with one row per accepted iteration, iteration 0 included.
4. **The covariance (POP-COV-01 to -04):**
   - R and the per-subject g_i by central differences;
   - the three kinds (`sandwich` printed by default, `r`, `s`), each with its own NC reasons;
   - `se_by_kind`;
   - the delta method to the user's scale (θ, the elements of Ω and the SD/correlations derived from them, ξ by FIT-ML-17);
   - RSE and correlations with eigenvalues and condition number.
5. **The result and flags (POP-EST-04):**
   - θ̂, Ω̂, ξ̂ (an `estimate` block, beside the `xi_used` that T-153 added for an evaluation), the η̂_i, the counts, the status, the trace and the covariance block;
   - the flags `at_bound`, `omega_collapse`, `high_eta_shrinkage`, `few_subjects`, `few_observations`, `slow_convergence`.
6. **Shrinkage and criteria (POP-DIA-03, -04):** η-shrinkage with divisor m₁ − 1, ε-shrinkage with N − 1, AIC and BIC with N observations and the parameter count of POP-DIA-04.
7. **The API the review asked for:**
   - an evaluation at an arbitrary φ with a warm-start map per subject;
   - a per-subject struct from the linearisation (E_i, the Cholesky factor of C_i, G, σ̂, pred and ipred), which T-155 will use for CWRES;
   - remove the temporary `not_available` code once the fit exists.
8. **The constants of OP-11** (ε_out, K_max, h, the tighter inner tolerance while differencing, the `omega_collapse` ratio, the slow-convergence count, the shrinkage level of the flag). Choose working values in code, named constants with a comment saying they wait for Q-026. Report each with what you measured to choose it: for h, the truncation and noise terms of POP-EST-03 on two cases.
