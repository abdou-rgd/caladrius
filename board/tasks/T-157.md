## Goal

Write `specs/simulation.md` from allowed sources. It describes simulation from a population model: given θ, Ω, the residual error model, a design (subjects, doses, times) and a seed, it returns simulated individual parameters, predictions and observations.

1. **The model:** the population model of `specs/population.md` (POP-MOD, POP-ERR). The structural models are `pk1.*`, and `pk2.*` in the clearance set. Is `pk3.*` included? Give a reason, since `specs/models.md` section 14 exists on `iter3/pk3` and may be merged.
2. **Randomness that is reproducible everywhere:**
   - **The generator:** name the uniform generator and the normal transform, as published algorithms with a citation.
   - **Determinism:** the same seed gives the same bits on 1 thread, N threads and wasm, as POP-DET-01 to 04 say for the fit.
   - **The streams:** how they are split per subject so that the result does not depend on threads, and how a subject's draw stays the same when other subjects are added.
   - **Correlated η:** draws under Ω by its Cholesky factor, with the factor's convention stated.
3. **What is simulated, and in what order:**
   - η, then individual parameters, predictions, ε, observations;
   - BLQ, if the design has an LLOQ;
   - the handling of a negative simulated value under additive error.
4. **The outputs:** per subject and per time. Which summaries are given, if any: quantiles over replicates, as in a VPC, with the quantile definition named (DAT or NCA conventions, if they exist).
5. **What can be tested, and how:**
   - **Exact parts:** with η and ε given, the observation is a deterministic function. That is an oracle at 256 bits, or through the existing model oracles.
   - **Distributional parts:** the moments and quantiles of the draws, against known values with a stated binomial or χ² bound, and a fixed seed.
   - **Bit-for-bit:** the generator itself against published test vectors of the chosen algorithm.
   - **Round trip with the fit:** simulate, then fit. This is the sealed design of POP-ORA-04, made by our own simulator.
6. **Refusals:** an invalid Ω, a missing seed, a design the model refuses, and limits on sizes.
7. **The VPC** (T-160, if time allows): what a VPC adds on top of simulation, in a short section marked `later` if it is not in v1.
8. **Open items for Abdou,** each with a proposal.

Every rule gets an id (`SIM-*`) and a grade (`assumed`, `documented, untested`), as in the other specs. Write worked examples with numbers computed in R 4.5.2, seeded, as hand checks. "Aucun chiffre inventé": every number comes from a computation you ran, and its script lives in the session scratchpad.

  - **Item 2, randomness (SIM-RNG-01 to 08, SIM-DET-01 to 03):**
    - The uniform generator is Philox4x32-10 (Salmon et al. 2011): counter-based, integer only, safe on wasm and published with test vectors.
    - The counter holds the block index, `purpose << 24 | replicate`, and the first 8 bytes of the SHA-256 of the subject label. The key is the seed.
    - So adding, removing or reordering subjects, or changing the thread count, never moves another subject's draws. A run of R replicates equals the first R of a longer run.
    - The uniform is u = ((x >> 12) + 0.5)·2⁻⁵², so 1 − u is exact and u is never 0 or 1.
    - The normal transform is the inversion of Wichura's AS 241, one normal per uniform. Box-Muller and rejection methods were set aside because the position of a draw would depend on values.
    - η = L·z, with L the lower Cholesky factor (Banachiewicz order). A pivot ≤ 0 gives `omega_not_positive_definite`.
    - The engine does not reproduce R's Mersenne Twister, so a simulation is not expected to match R at the same seed.
  - **Items 3 to 7:**
    - **Order (SIM-ORD-01 to 06):** η, then P, then the predictions, then σ, ε and y, then the negative rule (`keep` by default, or `zero`), then BLQ (y < LLOQ strictly, exported as `cens` = 1 with DV = LLOQ). ε is drawn even when σ = 0 or the row is BLQ.
    - **Outputs (SIM-OUT-01, 02; SIM-SUM-01):** tables, a dataset export, and quantiles over replicates by Hyndman-Fan type 7 in the form `(1-g)*x_lo + g*x_hi`.
