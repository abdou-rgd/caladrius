# T-100: pass^k next to the per-turn rate: an exercise counts only if it is right on every seed

- **Role:** apothicaire (repository abdou-rgd/apothicaire, `../agent` locally)
- **State:** in progress
- **Depends on:** T-083, T-099
- **Source:** literature watch of 2026-10-20 (Library `veille/`; pass^k from tau-bench, Yao et al. 2024), accepted by abdou on 2026-10-21; `../agent/bench/FROZEN_SET.md` §6.3

## Goal

A per-turn rate hides an assistant that is right once and wrong on the next seed. Report, per configuration, pass^k = C(c, k) / C(n, k) per exercise (c seeds passed out of n), averaged over exercises, for k = 1 to n, next to the per-turn rate with its cluster bootstrap interval. pass^n is the strict endpoint of §6.3 (the 8 turns CORRECT on every seed); exercises with mixed outcomes across seeds are listed as repeatability. The paired v1/v2 comparison of §11.3 (exact McNemar on exercises, mean d with a paired bootstrap, sign-flip test) sits beside it, descriptive in the demo tier.

## Allowed files

`../agent/bench/{stats.py,run_frozen.py}`, `../agent/tests/{test_bench_stats.py,test_bench_frozen.py}`, the card.

## Completion criterion

Unit tests check pass^k against hand-computed values, the strict rule (a missing seed fails the exercise) and the paired comparison on synthetic records; `run_frozen.py analyse` prints pass^1 to pass^3 next to the per-turn rate.
