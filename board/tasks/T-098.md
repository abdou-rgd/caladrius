# T-098: Units: a dose per body weight (mg/kg, µg/kg) gives CL in L/time/kg and volumes in L/kg

- **Role:** engine (caladrius-nca units), interface (caladrius-project derived units and warnings, schema text)
- **State:** to review
- **Depends on:** none
- **Source:** out-of-sample coursework test of 2026-10-21 (data kept outside the repository): paediatric doses are given in mg/kg; Caladrius refuses `mg/kg` as an NCA dose unit, so the dose has to be declared `mg` and CL and volumes come out labelled `L/h` and `L` instead of `L/h/kg` and `L/kg`

## Goal

`specs/nca.md` NCA-UNIT-01 already says the dose unit may be an amount per body weight and that units propagate formally ((mg/kg)/(h·mg/L) reduces to L/h/kg), with status `documented, untested`. The code does not follow it: `crates/caladrius-nca/src/units.rs` accepts only a mass, and `crates/caladrius-project/src/units.rs` warns `dose_per_body_size` and asks for the absolute dose.

Implement the rule for a mass per kilogram (`ng/kg`, `µg/kg` and its spellings, `mg/kg`, `g/kg`):

- NCA: CL in `L/<time>/kg`, Vz and Vss in `L/kg`, dose-normalised values per `(mg/kg)`; the numbers are unchanged (the factor comes from the mass part only);
- worksheet derived units and the mass-mismatch check read the mass part; no `dose_per_body_size` warning for a per-kg dose;
- a dose per body surface (`/m2`) keeps the warning: not covered by this card.

## Allowed files

`crates/caladrius-nca/src/units.rs`, `crates/caladrius-nca/tests/units.rs`, `crates/caladrius-project/src/{units.rs,tests.rs}`, `crates/caladrius-engine/src/schema.rs`, `specs/nca.md` (NCA-UNIT-01 status and wording), the card.

## Completion criterion

`cargo test -p caladrius-nca -p caladrius-project -p caladrius-engine` passes with new tests: the same synthetic profile run with dose `mg` and dose `mg/kg` gives identical numbers and the per-kg labels; `µg/kg` against `ng/mL` gives the same factor as `µg` against `ng/mL`; `mg/m2` is still refused by the NCA and still warned by the worksheet. Conformance unchanged (`cargo xtask conformance`: 48955 of 48955). Clippy, layers, lint green.
