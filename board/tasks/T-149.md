- 2026-11-07 orchestrator, decisions on the review:
  - **A1 is accepted, with a narrower rule than the reviewer's "every y = 0".** The narrower rule: on a route with a lag, under `combined`, `combined_sd` or `proportional`, a row with y = 0 is refused as `unbounded_likelihood` before fitting when both hold:
    1. **It is a leading zero:** every observation at a time ≤ its time is also 0.
    2. **The domain of tlag reaches its time:** tlag has no upper bound, or an upper bound ≥ that time. A fixed tlag counts as both bounds.
    - **Why leading zeros only.** With tlag at that time, every earlier row is predicted 0 too. Any nonzero earlier observation then makes −2LL go to +∞ as σ goes to 0, so a zero after a nonzero observation does not make the likelihood unbounded through the lag.
    - **What stays the same:** the t = 0 rule of eae738c stands, and so does `NonPositiveSigma` at the start.
    - **The sentence** names the rows. It offers four ways out: exclude the rows, bound or fix `tlag` below them, or use `additive`.
