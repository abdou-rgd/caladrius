## Goal

The recall bench holds 8 short facts (50 to 93 characters), kept verbatim under the 160-character threshold (`../optchat/bench.py`, FACTS; `../optchat/optchat.py:37`, `:362-368`). It has no tool call and no value that could be confused between subjects or analyses. Its regexes also accept wrong answers: "postgresql, parce qu'elle n'a pas de transactions" and "ce n'est pas 650 euros" both pass. Merges at L1 and above do go through the model (`:405`, `merge_pairs`).

Build a bench whose facts are NCA results of several subjects and analyses, recalled after compaction. Score value, unit, subject and analysis, and reject negations.

## Allowed files

`../optchat/bench.py`, a new results file next to it, `../optchat/README.md`, the card.

## Completion criterion

The bench runs on the PC with the 27B and reports one row per fact; the two wrong answers above score false.
