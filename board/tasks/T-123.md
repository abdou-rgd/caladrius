## Goal

The site of officina-dmpk.com takes the direction of the mockup: a workbench of an apothecary of Algiers around 1740 (Ibn Hamadouche, *Kashf al-rumuz*, Leclerc's translation), drawn in WebGL, whose objects open the sections: mortar (the engine), balance (simulation), manuscript (documentation), alembic (the assistant), jars (datasets). Night, brass and paper palette; Amiri, Alegreya Sans, IBM Plex Mono. To settle in the brief, from a reading of the mockup on 2026-10-29:

- every figure on the page comes from Caladrius (the engine in WebAssembly, as `/app/` does), never from page script: the mockup's balance computes its curve and its Cmax, tmax and AUC in JavaScript;
- the content follows the repositories: no three-compartment model (one and two only), links to `abdou-rgd/` (the mockup links the empty `officina-dmpk` organisation), the real CLI syntax;
- the assistant's example never computes, and never recommends a dose: the mockup's answer gives "about 1 g every 12 h";
- a version without WebGL for phones, reduced motion and screen readers, with the same text;
- the logo's green and the palette of the mockup: one identity, decided in iteration 1.
