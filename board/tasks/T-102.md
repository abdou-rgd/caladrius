# T-102: Apothicaire: accept a dose per kg of body weight (mg/kg) and carry it to the units of CL and V

- **Role:** apothicaire (repository abdou-rgd/apothicaire, `../agent` locally)
- **State:** todo
- **Depends on:** T-098 (done: Caladrius labels CL `L/<time>/kg` and volumes `L/kg` for a per-kg dose)
- **Source:** follow-up of T-098, out-of-sample coursework test of 2026-10-21 (data kept outside the repositories); accepted by abdou on 2026-10-21

## Goal

Caladrius now takes `mg/kg` as the dose unit of an NCA, but the agent drops it: `nca_units()` (`../agent/apothicaire.py:208-217`) accepts only a dose unit in `UNIT_MASS`, and the `dose_unit` argument shown to the model is the enum `ng, ug, mg, g` (`:166`). A per-kg dose therefore gives a unitless NCA, and the answer cannot name CL and V with their units.

- `dose_unit` and the worksheet dose unit may be a mass or a mass per kg (`ng/kg`, `ug/kg`, `mg/kg`, `g/kg`); `nca_units()` passes it to Caladrius unchanged.
- The unit labels come from Caladrius (`unit` of each parameter, `derived_units` of the worksheet): `param_unit()` and the `.dn` labels must show `L/h/kg`, `L/kg` and `(<unit>)/(mg/kg)` without building them.
- Gate v1 and gate v2: a value quoted with its per-kg unit passes; a value converted to another unit (for example mL/min/kg, or the absolute L/h) is still rejected (`gate_v2.py:575` unit-conversion check).

## Allowed files

`../agent/apothicaire.py`, `../agent/gate.py`, `../agent/gate_v2.py`, `../agent/tests/`, the card.

Bench gold and the 25 exercises unchanged (no exercise uses a per-kg dose), so no bench re-run is required.
