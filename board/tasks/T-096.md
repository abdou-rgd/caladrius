## Goal

Lines over 512 bytes are cut at the byte, mid-word (`../optchat/optchat.py:350`), and the compactor sees only the head of a long message (`:354-360`), so the end of a tool output is lost.

## Allowed files

`../optchat/optchat.py`, `../optchat/tests/`, the card.

## Completion criterion

`clip_line()` cuts at a word boundary with an ellipsis, never inside a UTF-8 character; `head_tail()` keeps 2/3 head and 1/3 tail; tests; recall bench v7 re-run on the PC.
