## Goal

Today the population oracle checks the objective at fixed parameters, the identities and the refusals. It has no ground truth for the estimates themselves.

For the `identity_linear` model of POP-MOD-05 with additive error, the model is linear in η and σ does not depend on η. So FOCEI is the exact marginal likelihood of a linear mixed model. When every subject has the same times (a balanced design), its maximum is known in closed form, or up to a one-dimensional search.

1. **Cases:**
   - q = 1 (random intercept);
   - q = 2 (intercept and slope), with Ω diagonal and with Ω a block;
   - at least one design whose maximum lies on the boundary (Ω̂ not positive definite), as a recorded refusal or flag, whichever the spec says.
2. **Three references that must agree:**
   - the closed form, at 256 bits;
   - `nlme::lme(method = "ML")`, which is open and installed;
   - a direct maximisation of the exact objective of POP-OBJ.

   Record the worst disagreement for each case.
3. **What is asserted:** θ̂, Ω̂, σ̂ and the objective at the maximum, with tolerances proposed from the measured disagreement and named in the report. Standard errors are recorded, not asserted.
4. **Failing tests first:** the cases go into `crates/caladrius-fit/tests/oracle_pop.rs` (or a sibling file) and fail with `not_built` until T-154.

5. **The two nits of the T-152 re-check:**
   - the doc of `POP_ETA` in `crates/caladrius-testkit/src/pop.rs` (about lines 78 to 81): it gives "1.3 times `POP_OFV`" and "a factor 0.7", which read as a contradiction. Say instead that the q = 3 worst case would be 1.3 times `POP_OFV`, so the margin there is below 1 (a factor 0.7).
   - `the_reference_is_complete_for_the_tests` in `oracle_pop.rs` checks the subject counts of `POP_EXACT_ENGINE_CASES` only. Add `POP_EXACT_FOCE_CASES` to that loop.
6. **The data** is H38 in `specs/sources.md` and the fourth family of POP-ORA-01 (30 values, seed 38001). Rebuild it from the text and check it against the table before any fit.
