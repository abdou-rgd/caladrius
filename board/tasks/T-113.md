## Goal

`AGENTS.md` states the motto, tabulates every role with its model, its effort and the reason for the choice, says that a subagent's report is a claim the orchestrator re-runs, and defines the iteration as a board card above the task cards, with a brief before any work, an audit, an outcome with a bolder next step, and a learning page. The file stays under 200 lines.

## Decisions taken

- **An iteration is a card**, `- **Kind:** iteration`, not a new file such as `docs/ITERATIONS.md`: the board stays the only memory. Its cards say `- **Iteration:** T-xxx`.
- **`reviewer` moves from Sonnet to Fable.** It reviews the numerical code that Opus writes; the reviewer should reason at least as hard and be another model. Budget: only the cards of the numerical crates and the iteration audit, a diff and its card each time, one Fable agent at a time; a card outside the numerical crates may be reviewed with `model: sonnet`.
- **The orchestrator runs on Opus**, not Fable: it holds the longest context, and two Fable sessions used about 44 % of the quota measured on 2026-10-19.
- **`onizuka` becomes a role**: it writes the learning page of each iteration (T-105 had kept it outside section 8).
- **Section 12 (marching order) goes**: steps 0 to 6 are done, and section 1 already gives the order of what remains. Rule 9 loses "advisory until step 6": CI runs `cargo xtask wasm` as a gate.
