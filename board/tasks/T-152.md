1. **The exact identification suite (POP-ORA-01), at 256 bits with Rmpfr, needing no population software.** Inputs are decimal strings, and each case writes its expected values as decimal strings.
   - **(a) Linear models with additive error** (PX1 and a few more): the marginal likelihood is exact, and all forms agree with it to 256-bit rounding. Include q = 1 and q = 2, and a diagonal Ω and a block Ω.
   - **(b) Nonlinear models with additive error** (PX5 and a few more, from `pk1.*` closed forms written in R):
     - `linearised`, `laplace_no_variance` and `laplace_expected` are equal to rounding (the identity of POP-OBJ-04);
     - `laplace_full`, FO and the exact marginal differ from them.

     Compute the exact marginal by high-precision quadrature for q = 1, and say how its error is bounded.
   - **(c) The frozen-σ identity:** proportional or combined error with σ held at its value at the mode, which is plain FOCE (OP-14 is open). `linearised` equals `laplace_no_variance` to rounding. Write it as a check of the formulas, whatever Abdou decides on OP-14.
   - **(d) With interaction:** the same data with σ depending on η, where the forms split (PX2). Record each form as its own expected value, so that the engine's default form is checked and the others are recorded.
2. **Objectives at fixed parameters, nonlinear, in double precision** (PX2 and more).
   - **Models:** `pk1.iv_bolus`, `pk1.oral_1` and `pk2.iv_bolus` in the clearance set, with each of the four error models.
   - **Data:** seeded synthetic data, plus Theoph (`oracle/data/theoph.csv`, 12 subjects, `pk1.oral_1`) and Indometh (`oracle/data/indometh.csv`, `pk2.iv_bolus`).

   - **The R script must not share the engine's inner algorithm.** Find η̂_i by a different route, for example `optim` on h_i from several starts, then a Newton polish. Check the stationarity at higher precision, to a stated size.
3. **Diagnostics (POP-DIA-01 to 04), on the cases of item 2** (PX3 and more): PRED, IPRED, IWRES, and CWRES in the three forms (diagonal, Cholesky in the time order of DAT-ORD-01, symmetric).
