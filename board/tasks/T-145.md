## Goal

T-144 puts the six `pk3.*` models (MOD-3C-25) in `caladrius-models` and routes them in `caladrius-fit`. Nothing outside those crates knows them yet, so the engine commands, the CLI and the MCP server still refuse `pk3.*`. Make them reachable, as T-034a did for `pk2.*`:

1. **The engine** (`caladrius-engine`):
   - the `ModelId` schema and the model catalogue list every `pk3.*` id, with the three parameter sets of MOD-3C-02 to 05 as the spec and the oracle name them;
   - `describe()` describes them;
   - `model.simulate`, `fit.run`, `fit.evaluate` and the model commands accept them;
   - `fit.initial_estimates` returns the readable refusal of `initial.rs` (no automatic estimates for three compartments) through the command, not as an internal error;
   - a refusal from MOD-3C-06, 08 or 26 reaches the caller as its sentence.
2. **The CLI and the MCP server:** the same ids, in their help and tool descriptions. Say by how much the MCP tool schema grew.
3. **`xtask conformance`:** read `oracle/expected/models/pk3/` as it reads `pk2/`, and write the three-compartment rows into `docs/conformance.md`. The 47 `scalar_ill` cases stay out of the count until Q-024 is answered; say how they are reported.
4. **The digest.** If it moves, list every step whose answer changed and why. Only answers that list or describe models may change.
