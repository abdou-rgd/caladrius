## Goal

Write the specification of the three-compartment models before any code, as T-031 did for two compartments: add section 12 "Three-compartment models" to `specs/models.md` (ids `MOD-3C-*`), in your own words and formulas, from the allowed sources only (`AGENTS.md` rule 1 and section 6: Gibaldi & Perrier, Gabrielsson & Weiner, Bertrand and Mentré 2008 already in `specs/sources.md`, papers such as Abuhelwa, Foster and Upton 2015, the documentation of open tools). Never a commercial program's documentation.

1. The system: central compartment and two peripheral compartments, micro-constants k10, k12, k21, k13, k31, and the parameterisations: clearances (CL, V1, Q2, V2, Q3, V3), micro-constants with V1, and macro-constants (A, B, C, α, β, γ). Conversions in both directions, the domains (α > β > γ > 0) and what is an error.
2. The three exponents are the roots of a cubic. Give a numerically stable way to compute them (the trigonometric form or another, with its conditioning when two roots are close), using only operations available in `libm` (rule 10).
3. Closed forms for every route of `pk2.*`: IV bolus, IV infusion (during and after), first-order absorption with and without lag (the limits ka = α, β or γ and stable evaluation near them), zero-order absorption with and without lag. Section 11 writes every pk2 function as a positive combination of one-compartment functions (MOD-2C-06); say whether the same holds with three weights, and use it if it does.
4. Derived quantities: the three half-lives, AUC, AUMC, MRT, Vss, Vz, Cmax and Tmax (closed or numerical), consistency with NCA.
5. Partial derivatives in the internal parameters (the analogue of ψ of MOD-2C-17) and the chain to the input parameters (ka, dur, tlag) and to the default parameterisation, including the derivatives of the roots by implicit differentiation of the cubic.
6. Multiple dosing and steady state: what the regimens of T-049 need from a pk3 function, and whether superposition and the steady-state sums of section 8 apply unchanged.
7. Model ids (`pk3.iv_bolus`, `pk3.iv_infusion`, `pk3.oral_0`, `pk3.oral_0_lag`, `pk3.oral_1`, `pk3.oral_1_lag`) with the default parameterisation, as MOD-GEN-05 and section 11 do.
8. Numerics and refusals: near-degenerate roots, overflow, what the engine must refuse.
9. Open items for the oracle (OM-xx): the closed-form grids at 256 bits, the ODE cross-check (`deSolve`), derivatives against high-precision central differences (as H14), refusals, multiple dosing and steady state.

Every rule carries a status (`documented, untested` or `assumed`) and its sources with page or section. No copied sentence.
