# T-109: Plot the raw data of a worksheet before any analysis

- **Role:** interface (caladrius-ui)
- **State:** to review
- **Depends on:** T-103, T-107
- **Source:** abdou, 2026-10-22: advice of the pharmacokinetics teacher, "always plot the data before an NCA", to see the shape of the curves and know what to expect

## Goal

As soon as a worksheet exists (imported from a CSV or typed and pasted, T-103), and before any analysis, its screen shows the concentrations as entered against time, on a linear and a semi-log axis side by side (stacked when the window is narrow). One line, one colour and one marker shape per subject; points below the limit of quantification marked apart (hollow triangle: at 0 on the linear plot, under the lowest decade on the semi-log plot); a legend whose entries hide or show a subject; under the pointer, the subject, the time and the concentration with their units; axis titles with the units of the worksheet. Read only: no PK computation, no regression, no interpolation, no engine change; the points are the cells of the worksheet table (`export.table`, BLQ cells written `BLQ`). Same code in the browser.

## Allowed files

`crates/caladrius-ui/src/{rawplot.rs (new),sheet.rs (one call),plot.rs (visibility of Axes::plot, paint_grid, dot_size, weight),theme.rs,theme.json (series_subjects),lib.rs}`, `crates/caladrius-ui/examples/snapshot.rs`, the board.

## Completion criterion

`cargo test -p caladrius-ui` green, including the 9 tests of `rawplot` (series per subject in order of appearance and time order, BLQ and skipped rows, units on the axes, no subject column, missing role, colours and shapes distinct for 40 subjects from the theme, marks stand out from the plot background, BLQ position and axis margins, hover text, legend click hides and "Show all" restores a subject on the drawn worksheet screen); `cargo clippy -p caladrius-ui --all-targets -- -D warnings`, `cargo xtask layers`, `lint`, `review`, `wasm` clean; snapshot scenes `04a`/`04b` (six invented subjects, light and dark) looked at. `docs/conformance.md` unchanged.
