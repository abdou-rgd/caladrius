## Findings

### F2 (Major, introduced by be71870): the 2^-900 fallback engages inside the accepted range and loses up to 3.9e-4; its justification is false

`params3.rs:518-526` (`macro_roots`): `let floor = f64::from_bits(123 << 52);` (= 2^-900 = 1.18e-271) with the comment "below FLOOR their low parts are subnormal". The low part of a double-double is about hi·2^-53, so it becomes subnormal when |hi| < 2^-969 (2^-1022 · 2^53), not below 2^-900. At hi = 2^-900 the low part is about 2^-953 and carries its full 53 bits. The threshold is therefore 69 binades too high, and in the band 2^-900 > |N0.hi| ≥ 2^-969 be71870 abandons an exact path for plain doubles while `from_macro` still accepts the set.

Measured, poles k21 and k31 of a micro set (k10 = 0.9, k12 = 0.5) scaled by 2^-s, converted to the engine's macro set and back (the `poles_*` sweep; `dd` = the Dd path was taken):

|---|---|---|---|---|
| τ = 1.07e-3 (`tau2^-10`) | 2^-450 | 2.8e-16 (dd) | 2.8e-16 (dd) | 2 |
| | 2^-451 … 2^-505 | 2.8e-16 (dd) | **k13 1.69e-11, k12 9.4e-12, k21 4.0e-15, k31 4.1e-15** (fallback) | 2 |
| τ = 1.05e-6 (`tau2^-20`) | 2^-451 … 2^-500 | k21/k31 3.97e-11 (dd, see F1) | **k13 3.86e-4, k12 2.14e-4, k21/k31 9.0e-11** (fallback) | 2 (k12, k13: 1.2e6, 2.2e6) |

Measured on 9d90144 (the Dd path with no floor): `r0.2` holds 3.0e-16 down to 2^-505 (n0 ≈ 2^-1002) and loses at 2^-510 (1.1e-15, n0 = 1.78e-308, now refused as "n0") and 2^-515 (3.2e-12); `tau2^-10` holds 2.8e-16 down to 2^-500 (n0 ≈ 2^-1000) and loses at 2^-505 (5.9e-14), 2^-510 (8.8e-11). So the true onset is at |N0.hi| ≈ 2^-969, as the arithmetic says, not 2^-900.

Smallest fix: `let floor = f64::from_bits(54 << 52);` (2^-969, the value at which hi·2^-53 reaches `f64::MIN_POSITIVE`) and a comment that states that criterion; add a unit test with the poles of a micro set at 2^-460 and τ ≈ 1.1·2^-10 asserting k21, k31, k12, k13 within 1e-12 of the micro set (be71870 gives 1.7e-11 on k13 and fails; the Dd path gives 2.8e-16). ### F1 (Major, pre-existing on 9d90144, same on be71870): `macro_roots` drops the rounding residual of β + γ and breaks the k21/k31 obligation for small poles at τ near 2^-20

`params3.rs:507-509`: `let sum = Dd::sum(alpha, beta).add_f(gamma);` and `term` forms S − λi as `sum.add_f(-minus)`. When β and γ are both below ulp(α)/2, `Dd::sum(alpha, beta)` is (α, β) exactly and `.add_f(gamma)` gives (α, fl(β + γ)): the rounding residual of β + γ (relative 2^-53 of β + γ) is lost before anything is subtracted, and S − α comes out as fl(β + γ) with only 53 correct bits. That error enters N1, then Δ·T² = N1² − 4·N0·T, where it is amplified by 1/τ²: 2^-53 · 2^40 ≈ 1e-4.

Measured (`poles_tau2^-20`, zone (ii), Dd path taken, both commits identical): at pole scale 2^-0 every constant is within 2.4e-16; from 2^-53 (onset checked at 2^-52: 3.8e-16; 2^-53: 1.70e-4) to 2^-450: k21 and k31 off by **3.97e-11** (κ = 2 and 1.6: the 1e-12 obligation of MOD-3C-08 zone (ii) is broken), k12 9.5e-5 (κ 1.2e6, not held), k13 1.70e-4 (κ 2.2e6, not held). `tau2^-10` does not show it (2.8e-16 throughout) because 2^-53/τ² ≈ 1e-10 stays under k12/k13's own conditioning and k21/k31 take only 2^-53/τ ≈ 1e-13. On the Dd intermediates (`t171_roots_probe`): Δ·T² relative error 1.5e-20 for s ≤ 32 and 7.57e-5 for s ≥ 64; the emulation (`dd_emul.py`) reproduces 7.6e-5 and gives N1 4.2e-17 → 2.0e-33 and Δ·T² 7.6e-5 → 6.6e-21 when S − λi is formed as the double-double sum of the two other exponents.

Smallest fix (one line): in `term`, `(Dd::sum(j, k).mul_f(w), Dd::product(j, k).mul_f(w))` and delete `sum` (unused). `Dd::sum` is exact (two_sum), so this is never worse than the current form and never cancels. Add a unit test with the poles at 2^-100 and τ ≈ 1.1·2^-20 asserting k21 and k31 within 1e-12 of the micro set (be71870: 3.97e-11, fails). Re-run the 3C oracle: accepted rows may move in their last bits.
