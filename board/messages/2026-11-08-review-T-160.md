## 3. Findings

Severity: M = fix before done, L = fix when convenient, I = information.

**F1 (M) BLQ-02 `censored_rank` band rule contradicts VX4 and STAT-03.**
vpc.md:193 and :118 say the band is over the replicates that have a number, and that a censored-rank replicate is left out. VX4 (vx4_blq.R, `q7_cens` applied to the replicate statistics with `nc` = replicates below) does the opposite: it keeps the below-LLOQ replicates as the lowest ranks of the band. That is why the band's lower end is "below LLOQ" at t=8, p=0.05 (85 of 100 replicates below). With the rule as written the band could never be below the LLOQ.
Fix: write the VX4 rule in BLQ-02 ("replicate statistics below the LLOQ are the nc lowest ranks of the band, same function as the observed one") and add the exception in STAT-03. Or change VX4. Pick one.

**F2 (M) PC-05 refuses routine data.** vpc.md:169.
An oral model has PRED(0) = 0. An observation at the dose time is allowed (DAT-ORD-01) and is not `before_dose`. With lb = 0 the whole pcVPC is refused (`non_positive_pred`). OV-08 offers "drop and count" only as an alternative.
Fix: say in PC-05 that this case is expected for oral data with a time-0 sample; make "drop the row and count it" the proposal in OV-08 or an explicit option. Add the case to ORA-04.

**F3 (M) SIM-03 contradicts POP-DAT-01.** vpc.md:34.
"The subjects are those used by the fit... a subject whose rows are all BLQ stays in". POP-DAT-01 says such a subject (n_i = 0) is not counted in m and is listed as left out.
Fix: "the subjects of the dataset, minus the user's exclusions; a subject with n_i = 0 stays".

**F4 (M) Which observed rows are BLQ when `lloq` differs from the data.** vpc.md:186.
The simulated flag is y < LLOQ(option). The observed flag is the dataset's `cens`. With an option different from the data limit the two fractions of BLQ-03 are not comparable, and the spec does not say whether an observed numeric value below the option is re-flagged.
Fix: state the observed flag (proposal: `cens` only, and refuse or note when the option differs from the limit), or re-flag by y < LLOQ.

**F5 (M) REF-01 has codes but no order, and misses option errors.** vpc.md:319-338.
The task asks for a code and an order per refusal; simulation.md fixes an order for its own. Several causes can occur together (e.g. `too_few_replicates` and `bad_edges`) and the oracle needs the one reported. Missing codes: bad `bins`, `bin_x`, `k` (k < 1; k = 1?), `min_obs`, `lb` (not finite), duplicate or empty π, unknown stratifier column. `dataset_changed` appears only in OV-13.
Fix: add an "order" column (inputs, fit, data, bins, PRED, size) and one `InvalidOption`-style code as in SIM-MOD-05.

**F6 (M) Simulate all rows, then drop (SIM-01 against SIM-02).** vpc.md:24 and :29.
SIM-MOD-04 item 3 simulates all observation rows (missing, BLQ and before-dose ones included) and the j-th row uses ε-word j-1. SIM-02 says these rows are "dropped from the VPC design". If dropped rows are not simulated, the ε positions shift and the counterpart of observed row j is no longer simulated row j.
Fix: one sentence in SIM-02: "the simulator is given every observation row; the rows are dropped after the simulation".

**F7 (L) Grades.**
- VPC-DET-01, -02 (vpc.md:272, :277) and ORA-05 (:314) are `documented, untested` but their only sources are SIM-DET-01 to -03, all `assumed`. A rule cannot be graded above its only source. Make them `assumed`.
- VPC-DET-03 (:282): the sequential double sum is a Caladrius decision; the measurement supports the reason, not the grade. `assumed`.
- BLQ-03 mixes grades but is explicit about it; fine.
- PC-01 `documented` rests on Eq. 1 read as an image (:151). I could not verify it.

**F8 (L) What PREDbin is.** vpc.md:149.
The spec says PREDbin is the median of the PRED_j of the bin. The 2011 abstract (snippet) says "the typical population prediction for the median independent variable in the bin", i.e. PRED evaluated at the median x, a different quantity when doses or covariates differ. I could not read the Methods. Ask the author to quote the sentence that defines PREDbin; if it is silent or different, PC-01 is `assumed` with the reason.

**F9 (L) Wording and citation details.**
- vpc.md:342: Bergstrand and Karlsson 2009 is AAPS J 11(2):371-380, not 11(3).
- vpc.md:293 and :358: "27 of 27 statistics" should read "27 checks (9 replicate-wise vectors, 9 lower and 9 upper limits)".
- vpc.md:128: "at least one replicate in each tail" is loose. Type 7 gives a band for any R ≥ 2. The rule is a floor of R·a ≥ 1 chosen by the author, not a necessity.

**F10 (L) Binning gaps.**
- The valid range of k is not stated (k = 1? k above the number of rows?). The loop `1:(k-1)` in `vpclib.R` is wrong at k = 1 (runs l = 1, 0); the spec rule (k-1 cuts) is fine. A single distinct x gives a bin with left = right; say what it is.
- The scripts cite stale ids (`VPC-REF-05`, `VPC-BLQ-03` for the censored quantile, `VPC-ORA-03` for calibration). The spec itself is consistent.

**F11 (L) Output tables.** vpc.md:239.
`flag` carries one value (`below_lloq`), but under `censored_rank` the observed, sim_lo and sim_hi can be below the LLOQ independently (VX4: observed below, band hi a number). The table cannot say which.
Fix: three booleans `obs_below`, `lo_below`, `hi_below`.

**F12 (L) VPC-STR-04.** vpc.md:227. `covariate_varies` is already refused by the dataset (DAT-ERR-05); say it is the dataset's refusal, not a second one.

**F13 (I) Housekeeping.** `specs/sources.md` (line 232) still says the VPC pages were not read and has no S-key for the new sources. The card forbade editing it; open a follow-up. SIM-LAT-01 keeps `Status: later`, which is right under "one sentence only".

## New rules against SIM-ORD-05 and the rest

- `blq_limit_above_lloq`: a censored row has only its limit L (DAT-CEN-02 (b)). With an option LLOQ ≥ L, the true value is below L ≤ LLOQ, so the row is known BLQ. With L above the option the row's status is unknown, so refusing is the only honest answer. Coherent with the strict rule y < LLOQ of SIM-ORD-05.
- `n_reflagged` and re-flagging numeric observed values by y < LLOQ: makes the observed fraction and the simulated fraction the same test. Note that the fit (POP-DAT-02) used those rows as ordinary observations; the spec's reason (compare like with like) is enough. It is in the header, the canonical text and OV-18. Coherent.
- `n_pred_dropped`: dropped after the simulation, before binning, observed and simulated, and out of the BLQ fraction and PREDbin (PC-04, BLQ-03). It only acts with `prediction_correction = pc`; the spec says "routine" but never says it is 0 without the correction (R5, trivial).
- The embedded simulation in DET-04 is run with `lloq` of the VPC and `quantiles` = π: coherent with SIM-DET-04 parts 1 to 6.

## Remaining findings

**R1 (L) Order of `non_positive_pred` against `no_bins`.** vpc.md:343 and :345, with PC-05 (:171) and ORA-04 (10).
PC-05 drops the rows "before the binning", then refuses when no row is left. The table puts `no_bins` (order 6, "no usable row") before `non_positive_pred` (order 7). When the correction drops every row, the rule "first in order" reports `no_bins`, but ORA-04 case (10) expects `non_positive_pred`.
Smallest fix: move `non_positive_pred` to order 5 or 6 before `no_bins`, or make `no_bins` say "no usable row before the correction".

**R2 (L) `sim_med` below the LLOQ has no column.** vpc.md:196 and :242.
BLQ-02 names `med_below`, and VX4 has a median "<LLOQ" (t = 8, p = 0.05), but OUT-02 and the canonical text (part 9) have only `obs_below`, `lo_below`, `hi_below`.
Smallest fix: add `med_below` to OUT-02, part 9 of DET-04 and the `vpc_quantiles` row list.

**R3 (I) VX7 "3 bins".** No script line prints it. Add one line to vx7_edges.R (`equal_count_edges(xs[pred(xs) > 0], 10)`) or drop the sentence.

**R4 (I) DET-04 and the simulator's `negative` option.** The embedded simulation is described by R, seed, `lloq` and `quantiles`; its `negative` option (SIM-MOD-05, default `keep`) is not named, so line 2 of the canonical text is only implied. Say "`negative = keep`".

**R5 (I) `n_pred_dropped` is 0 without the correction.** One clause in PC-05.
